The Tale

Diesel, Dirt & Code

I turn wrenches and run operations at my family's trucking company in the Southern Tier of New York — heavy haul, dump trailers, real deadlines. That's where I learned how small businesses actually work: the phone rings, the job gets done, the invoice goes out.

Now I build websites the same way by hand, in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — for local businesses that are great at their trade and invisible online. Business degree, equipment-operator's patience, and a builder's stubbornness.
